## 2. Long description（英文，直接貼）

> An alien ship blasts you into the ocean. When you surface, the shore is gone — only drifting trash remains. Good. Trash is material.
>
> Collect debris, press B to build on your floating platform. Every structure adds a tile, and the safe zone around your base grows with it. BOT docks send little yellow boats out to haul salvage while you explore. Upgrade your fins, oxygen tank, dive suit, magnet and harpoon. Take bounty contracts. Fit harpoon modules — pierce, blast, EMP. Build the spacecraft and launch.
>
> The deep sea fights back. Four leviathans, each with its own pattern: a sweeping prism beam, homing missile swarms, a boulder-sized charge, and a current field that drags you in. Fast, slow, homing and laser bullets are all telegraphed — learn the tells, dodge, punish.
>
> Then the mothership: dodge meteors during ascent, breach the hangar, survive waves of guards, and break a four-phase reactor core. Kill the core and the escape countdown starts — 90 seconds, and a Mirror Core appears: a copy of your own gear, one skill level higher. Beat it and you go home.
>
> Run length: about 75–105 minutes (model estimate; median ~75 — beta will confirm), with a real ending. After the credits: 3 challenge modes (one life / 45 minutes / bare hands) and NG+ up to 9 rounds. 24 achievements, 8 dynamic sea events, and accessibility options (reduce motion, colorblind assist, text size).
>
> No install, no network, no ads, no in-app purchases, no account. Progress stays in your own browser.

## 3. Feature bullets（6 條，itch 用）

1. **A base that grows** — every building claims a tile; the safe zone expands, heals you and pushes enemies out.
2. **Working automation** — collection nets, salvage works and BOT boats gather while you're away; turrets fire on their own.
3. **Four leviathans, four patterns** — prism beam, homing missiles, charge and current field; every attack is telegraphed.
4. **A finale with a twist** — four-phase core, then a 90-second escape and a Mirror Core with +1 skill.
5. **~75–105 minutes with an ending** — bounties, modules, events and bosses keep the middle from going quiet.
6. **Replayable** — 3 challenge modes, NG+ to 9, 24 achievements, accessibility settings.

## 7. FAQ（5 題，可貼長描述底部或 FAQ 區）

**Q1. 一輪多長？需要一次玩完嗎？**
A. 約 75–105 分鐘（模型推估，中位約 75；封測後定案），可以分段；進度存在瀏覽器，關掉再開會接續。

**Q2. 手機可以玩嗎？**
A. 可以，390×844 以上支援觸控（虛擬搖桿＋射擊鈕）；PWA 可安裝到桌面。平板亦可。

**Q3. 需要網路或帳號嗎？**
A. 不需要帳號、不連伺服器；首次載入頁面需要網路，之後 PWA 可離線。沒有廣告與內購。

**Q4. 很難嗎？有輔助嗎？**
A. 難度來自密度與走位，不是一擊必殺；所有招式都有前搖。可開減少動態、色盲輔助與字級 120%，另有 3 個挑戰模式給想更難的人。

**Q5. 有結局嗎？破完還有什麼？**
A. 有明確結局（母船核心＋90 秒逃生／鏡像 BOSS）。破完可玩 3 個挑戰模式、進 NG+（0–9），並收 24 個成就。
